a bit more about me

i'm based in houston, texas and have been studying online for a while now. drawing has been the constant throughout all of it, i've been sketching away since i was a kid and i haven't really stopped! everything else (the modding, the programming, the general obsessive curiosity) kind of orbits around that :D

finishing things is an ongoing challenge for me... my work in progress folder is embarrassingly full, but i'm getting better at calling something done instead of endlessly tweaking it

my artwork

character work

character design is where i spend most of my time. i like building out the details: the outfit, the expression, the little things that make a design feel like a real person

fan art

fan art is how i stay loose and just draw for fun without the pressure of it meaning anything. whatever i'm currently obsessed with tends to end up here eventually

original pieces

original work is the stuff i'm proudest of when it actually gets finished. these are the pieces that started as a concept in my head and made it all the way to something i'm willing to show people
